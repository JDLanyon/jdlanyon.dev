# Dead Bread (VR) - IGB388 University Project

**Dead Bread** (originally developed under the codename *Rescreetply*) is a VR zombie survival game created for **IGB388** at QUT. Players explore a shopping centre environment, scavenging for supplies while defending against hordes of zombies in immersive virtual reality.

## Gameplay

- **VR First-Person Shooter** - Fully immersive VR experience with motion controller support
- **Resource Management** - Collect supplies, ammunition, and consumables while exploring
- **Zombie Encounters** - Dynamic enemy spawning and combat mechanics
- **Shopping Centre Setting** - Navigate an indoor retail environment with multiple areas to explore

## Development Focus

- **VR Interaction Systems** - Motion controller input, player locomotion, and object manipulation via Unity's XR Interaction Toolkit
- **Enemy AI** - Zombie behaviour patterns, pathfinding, and combat encounter design
- **Resource Scavenging** - Supply collection mechanics and inventory management
- **Immersive Environment** - Atmospheric indoor retail level design optimised for VR presence

## Development Context

This project was developed as part of IGB388 - a university unit focusing on game development. The VR implementation explores immersive game design principles, including player movement, interaction systems, and enemy AI within a Unity-based VR framework.
